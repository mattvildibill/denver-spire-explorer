What is accurate?

The model contains 530 building groups and 1,916 roof sections across about 2.6 km². City roof outlines and height records preserve real scale, placement, and true north. Spire has the most architectural detail, including its podium, stepped crown, floor slabs and approximate balcony modules.

Materials & light

Photographed concrete, asphalt and brick; afternoon environment lighting; baked geometric ambient shading; and reflections of the modeled district. Spire mullions, window surrounds and curbs have physical depth. Close-range architectural modules are inferred, not surveyed.

What is approximate?

This is an explorable GIS reconstruction, not a perfect photogrammetric replica. Facades, balconies and some trees and lamps are procedural or inferred. Ground is level. Detailed sculptures, interiors, current storefronts, street furniture and the rebuilt 16th Street Mall are not fully reconstructed. Aerial imagery is from September 2023 and contains shadows and building lean; it is not a current survey.

Real Street View opens Google's own photography at the current camera location in a separate tab or app. It requires no API key from you. Google may show its own consent screen; coverage and date vary. No Google, Instagram or commercial map imagery is copied into this model.

Free sources & tools

  • City and County of Denver building outlines — roof geometry and height data, principally 2022 mapping.
  • OpenStreetMap contributors — streets, paths, landmarks, parks and mapped trees, ODbL. The source extract reports July 15, 2026.
  • USDA NAIP 2023, via Planetary Computer — public-domain 30 cm source imagery, photographed September 25, 2023. Resampled for the viewer; downloaded September 9, 2026.
  • Poly Haven · CC0 — Asphalt 02, Concrete Pavement 02, Brick Wall 001 and Kiara 6 Afternoon. Generic surface and lighting references, not Denver facade photographs.
  • Blender 4.5.3 LTS · Three.js 0.180.0 (MIT). No paid tiles, keys, accounts or runtime map service.

The offline download embeds the model, imagery, code and source credits. External photography links need internet. Open GIS data remains subject to its source terms and attribution.
